# Placement Prep Vocabulary

A beautifully designed, highly interactive vocabulary learning web application tailored specifically for placement exam preparation. 

## 🚀 Features

- **Curated Word List:** 79 essential placement words complete with simple meanings, formal definitions, and contextual examples.
- **Interactive Flashcards:** Click to flip cards and reveal detailed examples.
- **Text-to-Speech Audio:** Built-in pronunciation for every word (click the 🔊 icon) to help with interview rounds.
- **Study Mode:** A distraction-free learning interface with scaled-up flashcards and hidden navigation.
- **Smart Search:** Instantly search words, meanings, or examples (press `/` to quick-focus the search bar).
- **Bookmarks:** Save difficult words you want to focus on by clicking the star (☆) icon on any card.
- **Mastery System (SRS):**
  - Get a word right 3 times in quizzes to mark it as **"Mastered 🏆"**.
  - Mastered words are greyed out so you can focus on new words.
  - If you forget a Mastered word and get it wrong in a quiz, it loses points and returns to your review queue!
- **Detailed Quiz Reviews:** After finishing a quiz, get a complete breakdown of every question, your answer, and the correct meaning.
- **Perfect Score Celebration:** Achieve a 100% on any quiz and be rewarded with a fun confetti animation!

## 🧠 7 Dynamic Quiz Modes

Test your knowledge with multiple question types (Meaning, Definition, Fill-in-the-blank, Synonyms, and Antonyms).

1. **Full Quiz:** Test your knowledge on all 79 words.
2. **Quick 10:** A fast-paced 10-question sprint.
3. **Time Attack:** Answer as many questions as you can in exactly 60 seconds.
4. **Pressure 20:** 20 questions with a strict 15-second time limit per question.
5. **Smart Review (SRS):** An intelligent Spaced Repetition System that *only* tests you on words you haven't mastered yet.
6. **Bookmarked:** A dedicated quiz mode to practice only the words you've starred.
7. **Story Mode:** Test your comprehension by filling in the blanks within a short generated story.

### Custom Quiz Settings
- **Deferred Feedback:** By default, quizzes show you if you were right or wrong immediately after every question. You can toggle this off to fly through the quiz and get a detailed breakdown of your answers at the very end.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **HTML5:** Semantic and accessible structure.
- **CSS3:** Modern, dark-themed UI with glassmorphism, responsive grids, and smooth micro-animations.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** 100% client-side logic with `localStorage` for saving your mastery progress. No databases or heavy frameworks required.

## 🛠️ How to Run Locally

Because this project is entirely built with standard web technologies, there is no complicated build step or installation required.

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Double-click on `index.html` to open it in your default web browser.
3. That's it!

*(Note: The app saves your quiz scores and mastery data to your browser's Local Storage, meaning your progress is saved even if you close the tab!)*
